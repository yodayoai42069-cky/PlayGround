Kagerō High is an original comedy visual novel. Every character, still, and note of music in it was generated for this project — no borrowed art, no stock photography.

Story & direction
Original screenplay in nine parts
Art
Procedural vector anime stills, drawn at runtime in a bold ink-and-cel style
Character sprites
Parametric SVG puppets with blinking, lip-flap, and hair sway
Music & sound
Synthesised live with the Web Audio API
Engine
Hand-rolled visual novel interpreter

Content note: romantic comedy with slapstick peril, innuendo-free fanservice gags, and three downbeat endings. All characters are 18 or older. Yui is the protagonist's stepsister by adoption and shares no blood relation.

Tip: press Space to advance, H for backlog, Esc for the menu, and click the little seal on the name plate to replay a line's voice-less… nothing. It just looks nice.
