# PlayGround

## 🌸 Petal Town Hearts — an Early Aughts Anime Dating Sim

A complete browser dating sim (no build step, no dependencies — just open `index.html`).
You are a customizable fresh graduate with **$10,000** who moves to a town inhabited
**only by women**. Fall in love, date, marry, and grow a family that helps fund even
more dates, gifts and weddings.

### ✨ Features

- **Completely customizable main character** — gender (**Male or Female only**), 8 body types,
  8 skin tones, 5 face shapes, 18 hairstyles, 16 hair colors, 14 eye colors, 22 outfits,
  12 accessories, blush & freckles. Change your whole look **any time for a fee** at the Bishoujo Salon.
- **Anime portraits drawn in code** — a parametric SVG renderer (`js/portrait.js`) with
  cel shading, big sparkly aughts eyes and mix-and-match traits for every character.
- **Map system** — clickable town map with 11 locations (café, park, mall, beach, shrine,
  gym, library, office, salon, chapel, your apartment). Travel costs energy.
- **Side scroll buttons** — big ▲ PREV / ▼ NEXT buttons on the right rail scroll and select
  through every menu: customization options, gift lists, date options and more (arrow keys work too).
- **10 bachelorettes** with distinct personalities, jobs, gift tastes, hangout spots and dialogue.
- **Gifts & dates** — 10 gifts from candy to engagement rings, 9 date types from a free park
  stroll to a yacht cruise. Affection tiers from Stranger to Fiancée.
- **Marriage → Offspring** — marry at 100 ♥ at the Starlight Chapel, then try for a baby at
  home. Every child is a **random genetic blend of both parents** (each trait is picked from
  either the mother or the father, with rare mutations) and earns daily funds for the family.
- **Perk shop** — 7 levelled perks that speed up money and offspring generation:
  Golden Touch, Silver Tongue, Town Sweetheart, Savvy Shopper, Blessed Union,
  Family Business and Big Family (twins!).
- **Economy loop** — work shifts at Aozora Corp, collect passive income from wives and
  children, and run the family hustle to fund the next date.
- **Happy Life goal** — 5 wives and 8 children triggers the festival ending (the game keeps going).
- **Auto-save** to your browser's local storage.

### 🎮 How to play

1. Open `index.html` (or run `python3 -m http.server` in this folder and visit the page).
2. Pick your name and gender (male or female), then start your new life with $10,000.
3. Use the **▲ / ▼ buttons** on the right to scroll and select actions.
4. Travel the map, talk to the women you meet, gift their favorite things, take them on dates.
5. Work shifts to stay funded — marry at 100 ♥, start the family, invest in perks.
